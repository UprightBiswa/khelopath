# 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:25 Pain

I am a cyclist, and when an athlete wants to find government-supported opportunities, training centres, or understand where they are eligible to apply, the information is spread across different portals and systems.

If you do not already know how the system works, it is difficult to know where to start.

I wanted to solve one simple problem: what can I actually do next?

## 0:25-1:45 Product Journey

Show KheloPath:

1. Select sport
2. Enter location
3. Choose goal
4. See relevant opportunities
5. Check eligibility
6. Understand documents
7. Submit demo application
8. Track status

## 1:45-2:20 Differentiator

I had previously worked on a government-facing grievance platform where citizens could report issues using photographs and geolocation. That experience taught me that the difficult part is not collecting a complaint. The difficult part is what happens after submission: classification, routing, responsibility, and resolution.

KheloPath includes escalation when an application gets stuck.

## 2:20-2:45 Architecture

Next.js on Vercel, route handlers for backend APIs, Neon PostgreSQL for workflow data, Prisma for migrations, and OpenAI for intent understanding and plain-language explanations.

## 2:45-3:00 Honesty

This is an independent prototype. Government integrations, identities, and records are synthetic. The prototype demonstrates how the service could work safely at scale.
