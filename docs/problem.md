# Problem

Indian athletes often need to discover public sports opportunities, training centres, eligibility conditions, documents, application steps, and grievance paths across fragmented services.

For an athlete who does not already understand the government sports ecosystem, the hardest question is simple:

> What can I actually do next?

KheloPath focuses on that next action.

## Demo Persona

- Age: 21
- Sport: Cycling
- Location: Guwahati, Kamrup Metropolitan, Assam
- Goal: Find government-supported training or sports opportunities

## Design Principle

Every screen should clearly answer:

> What do I do next?
