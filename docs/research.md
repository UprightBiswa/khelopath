# Research Notes

KheloPath is not an official government product and does not claim partnership with any public body.

The prototype is based on a common public-service pattern:

- Information is distributed across multiple websites.
- Eligibility conditions are difficult to interpret.
- Application statuses often do not explain whether the user must act.
- Grievance systems need classification and routing, not just submission forms.

Further research should validate opportunity categories, official process owners, multilingual needs, and accessibility requirements with athletes and sports administrators.
